About
Infrastructure for the regulated AI era.

Cosmos AI LLC builds developer-first compliance tools for companies deploying AI in regulated industries. We start with the problems that create the most liability — loan decisions, hiring screens, clinical recommendations — and build the infrastructure layer that sits between AI systems and the damage they can cause.

Our first product, Pragma, is a real-time AI compliance firewall covering the EU AI Act, ECOA, EEOC, FCRA, CFPB, and a growing library of state regulations. It ships as a single API endpoint — no compliance team required, no six-month implementation.

We're a small team with deep conviction: the next decade of enterprise AI adoption will be shaped by whoever builds the best trust infrastructure first. And we're building it faster than anyone else.
