Privacy Policy

Cosmos AI LLC d/b/a Pragma  ·  Effective date: September 1, 2026  ·  Version 1.0

We built Pragma to help organizations stay compliant — that means we take data privacy seriously. This policy explains exactly what we collect, why, and what rights you have over your data.

1. Who Controls Your Data

The data controller is Cosmos AI LLC (d/b/a Pragma), a Texas limited liability company. For EU/UK users, Pragma acts as the data controller under GDPR and UK GDPR respectively. Contact: privacy@usepragma.co

What we do NOT store: The text of decisions you submit and the context values you pass are processed in memory to produce the compliance analysis and are not written to our database. Logs contain only anonymized metadata.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To deliver the service — authenticate you, run compliance evaluations, generate certificates and reports
  • To improve the service — aggregate, anonymized usage patterns (which categories are evaluated, overall risk distribution)
  • To bill you — process payments, send receipts, enforce plan limits
  • To communicate with you — welcome emails, compliance deadline reminders, payment confirmations, and support responses

We do not sell your data to third parties. We do not use your compliance data to train AI models without your explicit consent.

6. Data Retention

  • Account profile and compliance data: Retained for the life of your account and deleted within 30 days of account deletion.
  • Anonymized usage metadata: Retained indefinitely for service improvement. This data cannot be linked back to you.
  • Billing records: Retained for 7 years as required by tax law, even after account deletion.
  • Email logs: Retained for 90 days.

7. Your Rights

If you are in the EU, UK, or California, you have the following rights:

  • Access (Art. 15 GDPR / CCPA): Download a copy of your data at any time from Settings → Account → Export Data.
  • Erasure (Art. 17 GDPR): Delete your account and all associated PII from Settings → Account → Delete Account. This takes effect immediately.
  • Rectification (Art. 16 GDPR): Update your name and notification preferences in Settings. Email changes require re-authentication via Google.
  • Portability (Art. 20 GDPR): Your data export (Settings → Export Data) is provided in JSON format.
  • Opt out of marketing: Use the unsubscribe link in any email, or disable notifications in Settings.
  • Restrict processing: Contact us at privacy@usepragma.co.

We will respond to rights requests within 30 days. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority (e.g., the ICO in the UK, your national DPA in the EU).

8. International Data Transfers

Pragma is hosted in the United States. If you are in the EU or UK, your data is transferred to the US. We rely on Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) with our processors where required. Stripe and Google operate under their own transfer mechanisms.

9. Cookies and Tracking

Pragma uses session tokens stored in browser memory (not persistent cookies) for authentication. We use PostHog for product analytics using anonymized identifiers — no cross-site tracking. We do not use advertising trackers.

10. Children

Pragma is not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe we have inadvertently collected such data, contact us immediately.

11. Security

We use TLS in transit, encrypted storage at rest, and hashed identifiers in logs. Payments are handled entirely by Stripe — we never store card numbers. API keys are stored as SHA-256 hashes. Access to production systems is restricted to authorized personnel.

12. Changes to This Policy

Material changes will be communicated to your registered email address at least 14 days before they take effect.
